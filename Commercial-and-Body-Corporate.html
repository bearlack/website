Commercial pressure cleaning on the Gold Coast

First impressions matter — especially in high-traffic commercial environments. Clean pathways, stain-free concrete and well-maintained exterior surfaces show customers, tenants and staff that your property is cared for and safe to use.

We provide commercial pressure cleaning across the Gold Coast and surrounding regions, delivering fast, reliable and high-quality results for businesses, body corporates and facility managers. Whether it's routine maintenance or a one-off deep clean, we have the equipment and experience to handle large-scale surfaces efficiently.

What we clean

We specialise in restoring and maintaining large external surfaces, including:

  • Carparks & multi-storey parking facilities
  • Shopping centres & supermarket fronts
  • Strata & body corporate complexes
  • Walkways, pathways & high-traffic public areas
  • Warehouses, workshops & loading zones
  • Commercial buildings, shopfronts & forecourts
  • Service stations, drive-throughs & industrial sites
  • Schools, sporting clubs & council spaces

From small retail strips to full commercial complexes — no job is too big.

How commercial work is priced

Commercial jobs are quoted per square metre, but we don't publish a flat rate — because on a real site, the rate isn't the thing that decides the price. What matters is:

  • Water access — whether there's a usable supply on site or we're carting it in
  • Drainage and wastewater — whether run-off needs to be captured and managed
  • Traffic and pedestrian management — what needs closing off, and when
  • Access and height — levels, ramps, low clearances, and what safety gear the site calls for
  • Surface condition — a carpark that's never been done takes far longer than one on a maintenance cycle

For anything sizeable we'd rather come and look at it. You get a real number instead of a guess, and we get a job we've actually scoped. Site inspections are free and there's no obligation.

Why businesses and body corporates choose us

  • Commercial-grade equipment for fast large-area coverage
  • We remove dirt, mould, algae & grime at the root — not just the surface
  • Degreasers & professional chemicals available for oil, rust & heavy staining
  • Fully insured for commercial work & public liability, certificate available on request
  • Early morning, night or weekend scheduling available to avoid business disruption
  • One-off cleans, scheduled maintenance & contracted site care available
  • Direct invoicing to building managers, strata committees and facilities managers

Clean surfaces aren't just about appearances — they help reduce slip hazards, extend the life of concrete and improve customer experience.

Scheduled maintenance beats one-off cleans

Most of our commercial clients end up on a schedule rather than calling us when a carpark has got visibly bad. There are two reasons for that. The first is cost: a surface that's maintained never builds up the layer that takes the longest to remove, so each visit is quicker and cheaper than a rescue job. The second is liability — algae and oil film on a walkway or ramp is a slip risk, and a documented cleaning schedule is a much better position to be in than an ad-hoc one.

We can work to quarterly, biannual or annual cycles depending on traffic, and invoice per visit or on an agreed schedule.

Eco-friendly, safe & WHS compliant

We prioritise safe operation at all sites, including traffic control options, hazard management and safe washing practices around pedestrians and vehicles. Where possible, we use biodegradable detergents and controlled wastewater management to protect surrounding environments.
