<p>Tallebudgera and the valley behind it are a different job to coastal Gold Coast. Blocks are larger, there's far more tree cover, and a lot of properties have long driveways, sheds and outbuildings that all need doing at the same time. The salt problem largely disappears here — and gets replaced by an organic growth problem that's arguably harder to stay on top of.</p>
