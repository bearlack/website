How GC Shine started

Today we've cleaned over 200 Gold Coast properties, from single units and family homes through to supermarket carparks and body corporate complexes. Every job still gets the same attention.

Why we soft wash instead of blasting everything

Plenty of operators will point a high-pressure gun at anything. It's faster, and on the day it can look impressive. The problem is what it does underneath — cracked tiles, stripped coatings, water forced up under roof sheeting, render damaged, paint lifted.

Soft washing takes longer and needs proper chemistry, but it kills mould, moss, lichen and algae at the root rather than knocking it off the surface. The result stays clean far longer, and your roof and paintwork last as long as they're supposed to. High pressure has its place — on concrete, where it belongs.
